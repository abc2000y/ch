📖 How to Play & Controls
⛵ Dodge Rocks: Drag your boat (or use Arrow keys / WASD) to avoid incoming rocks.
🕸️ Cast Net: Click swimming or leaping fish to net them; click empty water or press Space to row faster.
🐟 Fish Points: Small underwater fish (#1–4) = 1 pt, Medium (#5–8) = 2 pts, Large (#9–12) = 4 pts; netting leaping fish gives double bonus (min 5 pts, up to 8 pts for large fish)!
⚓ Dock & Upgrade: Survive each stage's days without crashing to reach port; boats upgrade when advancing with enough points (25 Sailboat / 60 Speedboat / 110 Yacht).
