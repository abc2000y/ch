📖 How to Play & Controls
⛵ Dodge Rocks: Drag your boat (or use Arrow keys / WASD) to avoid incoming rocks.
🔱 Harpoon & 🕸️ Net: Stage 1 uses the Harpoon (45° left/right strike, Large Fish #9–12 only); Stage 2 uses the Cast Net; Stage 3 alternates daily; Stage 4 uses Cast Net only!
🐟 Fish Points & Stage Goals: Stages 1–3: Small (#1–4) = 1 pt, Medium (#5–8) = 2 pts, Large (#9–12) = 4 pts (leaping fish: 5–8 pts). Stage 4: Protected fish incur fines (can push score below 0), non-edible fish grant +1 pt, and edible fish award standard points. Stage clear goals: 99 / 188 / 399 / 599 pts.
⚓ Dock & Upgrade: Survive all days without crashing and reach the stage score goal to clear! Boats upgrade on clear at 55 / 100 / 200 / 400 pts.
